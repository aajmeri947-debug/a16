Lasagna Make-Ahead & Freezing

Lasagna is one of the best dishes to make ahead. Assemble it a day early, freeze it for a busy week, or keep slices ready to reheat.

  • 5steps
  • 6min read
  • Nov2026 review
  1. 1

    Assemble a day ahead

    Build the lasagna, cover it tightly and chill it for up to 24 hours. Cold lasagna needs a little longer in the oven, and the pasta softens slightly overnight, which many cooks think improves it.

  2. 2

    Freeze it whole

    Assemble the lasagna in a freezer-safe dish, but do not bake it. Cool everything first, then wrap in plastic and then foil, pressing out air. Label with the date. It keeps well for about three months.

  3. 3

    Freeze it in slices

    • Bake and cool the lasagna completely, ideally overnight in the fridge.
    • Cut into portions while cold, so they keep their shape.
    • Wrap each slice separately and freeze flat.
  4. 4

    Bake from frozen

    You can bake a frozen lasagna without thawing. Keep it covered with foil and bake at 375°F for about 75 to 90 minutes, then uncover for the last 15 minutes. It should be piping hot in the centre before serving.

  5. 5

    Reheat leftovers

    Add a spoonful of water or sauce to a slice, cover it and warm it in a 350°F oven for about 20 minutes, or in a microwave on medium power. Leftovers keep three to four days in the fridge.

kitchen notes

  • Use disposable foil pans to free up your baking dishes.
  • Write the bake time on the foil before freezing.
  • Thaw overnight in the fridge to cut baking time nearly in half.
  • Never refreeze lasagna that has been thawed.
