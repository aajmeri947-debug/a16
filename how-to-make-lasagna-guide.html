How to Make Lasagna

A classic baked lasagna is built from three parts: a slow tomato ragù, a creamy béchamel and sheets of pasta. Make each one well, layer them with care, and the oven does the rest.

  • 5steps
  • 6min read
  • Nov2026 review
  1. 1

    Make the ragù

    Soften chopped onion, carrot and celery in olive oil. Add minced beef or a mix of beef and pork, and brown it well. Stir in tomato paste, then crushed tomatoes and a splash of stock or milk. Simmer gently for at least an hour, until thick and rich.

  2. 2

    Make the béchamel

    Melt butter, whisk in the same weight of flour and cook for a minute. Slowly whisk in warm milk until smooth. Simmer until it coats a spoon, then season with salt and a little grated nutmeg.

  3. 3

    Prepare the pasta

    • Fresh sheets go straight into the dish.
    • Dried sheets can be boiled for a few minutes first, then laid on a towel.
    • No-boil sheets need a slightly saucier ragù to soften.
  4. 4

    Layer it up

    Spread a thin layer of ragù on the bottom of the dish so the pasta does not stick. Then repeat: pasta, ragù, béchamel, a little parmesan. Finish with pasta, béchamel, mozzarella and a generous blanket of parmesan.

  5. 5

    Bake and rest

    Cover with foil and bake at 375°F for about 25 minutes. Uncover and bake 20 to 25 minutes more, until bubbling and golden. Rest for 15 minutes before slicing, so the layers hold together.

kitchen notes

  • Use a ruler of sauce: a thin layer is better than a thick one.
  • Leave a little béchamel showing at the edges so they crisp.
  • A metal pan browns faster than glass or ceramic.
  • Slice with a sharp knife, then lift with a flat spatula.
